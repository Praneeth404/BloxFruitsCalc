# BloxFruitsCalc

A clean, fast, and free calculator for **Blox Fruits grinders** who are tired of guessing and just want numbers.

Plan your **levels** and **mastery** efficiently so you can spend less time thinking and more time grinding.

> ⚠️ Not affiliated with Roblox or Blox Fruits.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 📈 **Level Calculator**
  - Calculates total XP needed from your current level to a target level
  - Accounts for current XP progress
  - Tells you exactly how many runs you need

- 🗡️ **Mastery Calculator**
  - Calculates mastery XP required to reach your target mastery level
  - Helps plan unlocks for new moves efficiently

- 🎯 **Accurate Scaling**
  - Uses in-game-like exponential formulas for XP and mastery
  - No oversimplified guesses

- 🧼 **Clean UI**
  - Minimal, responsive, and distraction-free
  - Designed for both desktop and mobile
